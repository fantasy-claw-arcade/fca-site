5 Ways to Plan Your Fantasy Claw Arcade Visit Online

Elliot Cole · Nov 27, 2026 · 3 min read

Our website has been completely rebuilt to get you from “we should go to the claw arcade” to standing in front of the prize wall faster. Here are five things you can do before you leave the house, plus our brand-new app.

1. Book a claw arcade birthday party in minutes

Planning a party used to mean phone calls back and forth. Now you can do it all online. Head to Birthdays & Events, pick your arcade, choose a package, and select your date and time. A 50% deposit holds your spot. Las Vegas and Easton Town Center parties can be booked online at least three days in advance. For our Honolulu arcade or a custom party, send us a request and we’ll plan the details with you.

2. Know your token options before you arrive

Each arcade page lists token packages right at the top, starting at 50 tokens for $40 and going up to 300 tokens for $200. The bigger the pack, the more tokens you get per dollar. Most claw machines take just one token per try, so you can plan your budget before you walk in.

3. Check today’s hours at a glance

Each location shows its hours for today, along with special holiday hours when they apply. Mall hours often change around the holidays, so a quick check before you head out saves you a wasted trip.

4. Send a gift card instantly

Need a last-minute gift? Buy a gift card online and send it instantly. It’s redeemable at any Fantasy Claw Arcade. It works for birthdays, stocking stuffers, or anyone who can’t walk past a claw machine.

5. Stay ahead of every deal and event

Our Special Promotions page always shows what’s running now and what’s coming next, from seasonal offers to events like the Wizard’s Cup claw tournament at Fashion Show on Saturday, December 5. Tournament spots are limited, so sign up early.

Now live: the Fantasy Claw Arcade app

Our new loyalty app is here. It’s built around three reward tiers: Apprentice, Wizard and Legend. Your tier depends on what you spend with us over the past 12 months, so regular players move up as they keep coming back.

Download the app from the App Store or Google Play, and sign up with the same phone number you use at the register so your points carry over.

Ready to play? Find your arcade and we’ll see you at the prize wall.
