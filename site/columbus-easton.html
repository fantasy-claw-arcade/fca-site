What to expect

More than 70 places to play on the second floor of Easton's Station Building. Line up the claw, time your drop and go for plush, collectibles and high-value prizes.

Stop by the trade-up counter for collectible upgrades. Great for families, date nights and groups.

Don’t miss our Claw King, a giant machine where you control ten claws at once, all dropping together for ten chances to win in a single play.

New to claw machines? Grab a token pack at the counter. Packs run from $40 for 50 tokens to $200 for 300 tokens, and the bigger packs stretch your tokens further. Most games take just 1 token per try, and some take up to 4, depending on the prize. Our team is happy to show first-timers how it works.

What guests say

Variety is what Columbus guests mention most: a wide range of claws and prizes, from classic plush to collectibles and premium items. Players who know claw machines appreciate the authentic Japanese arcade style, where skill and timing matter. Guests describe our team as welcoming and helpful and the arcade as clean and well put together, and plenty of adults say they had just as much fun as the kids. It’s a hit for birthdays, celebrations and team building, too.

Finding us

We're on the second floor of the Station Building.

Parking is free in the West Garage or East Garage, on either side of the Station Building between Gramercy St and Easton Station.

Trade up at the counter

Every plush you win counts toward a bigger prize. Keep your wins, or bring them to our trade-up counter: 5 plush gets you a prize from the 5-point tier, 10 gets you the 10-point tier, and so on up to the 20-point tier. Stack up your wins and walk out with a premium plush or collectible you couldn’t win in one go.

A stop on your Easton day

Easton Town Center is one of Columbus’s go-to spots to shop, eat and hang out, and we’re right in the Station Building. Make us part of a shopping trip, a family day out or a date night: grab dinner, then finish with a few rounds on the claws. We’re easy to find from either Station Building garage, and parking is free. Easton is just off I-270 on Columbus’s northeast side, about 15 minutes from downtown and John Glenn International Airport.

Parties and groups

Birthday parties and group outings are welcome here, and they’re all about the games. There’s no room for tables and chairs at this location, so food isn’t part of parties here. Party packages for Easton are coming soon. In the meantime, tell us your date, group size and what you have in mind, and our team will put together a custom party for you. Private full-arcade rentals are available too.

Easton FAQ

Is there a claw machine arcade in Columbus, Ohio?

Yes. Fantasy Claw Arcade is on the second floor of the Station Building at Easton Town Center, with more than 70 places to play.

Is parking free?

Yes. Park free in the West Garage or East Garage, on either side of the Station Building between Gramercy St and Easton Station.

Is it good for team building?

Yes. Groups can book a party or rent the entire arcade for a private team event.

Is it fun for adults, or just kids?

Both. Many of our Columbus guests are adults, couples and friend groups.

How much does it cost to play?

You pay per try with tokens. Most games take just 1 token, some take up to 4 depending on the prize, and token packs at the counter run from $40 for 50 tokens to $200 for 300 tokens.

How does the trade-up counter work?

Each plush you win counts as one point. Trade 5 plush for a prize from the 5-point tier, 10 for the 10-point tier, 15 for the 15-point tier, or 20 for the 20-point tier.

Can I book a birthday party here?

Yes. Party packages for Easton are coming soon. For now, send us your date and group size through the party request form and we’ll put together a custom party. Parties here are games only, since there’s no room for tables and chairs.
