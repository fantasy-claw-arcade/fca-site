Arcade Attendant · AREA15, Las Vegas, NV

Gamekeeper (Arcade Attendant)

Status
Full and Part-Time / Hourly
Reports to
Store Manager
Location
AREA15, Las Vegas, NV

Position summary

The Gamekeeper is the frontline face of the arcade, responsible for creating a fun, welcoming experience for every guest. This role handles transactions, restocks prizes, maintains cleanliness, and assists with daily operations to keep the arcade running smoothly.

Key Responsibilities

  • Greet and assist guests with enthusiasm, professionalism, and a focus on outstanding customer service
  • Promote token packages, arcade promotions, and loyalty programs
  • Assist customers with operating claw machines and redeeming prizes
  • Operate the POS system for transactions and reloads
  • Answer guest questions and resolve issues promptly and professionally
  • Maintain a clean, organized, and safe arcade environment, including machines and store presentation
  • Restock plushies, prizes, and merchandise regularly
  • Monitor token usage and track inventory as needed
  • Report any technical or maintenance issues to management
  • Perform opening, mid-shift, and closing duties, including register counts and store prep
  • Create a fun and memorable experience that encourages repeat visits

Skills & Requirements

  • Friendly, upbeat, and confident communicator with strong customer service skills
  • Dependable, punctual, and detail-oriented
  • Ability to multitask and thrive in a fast-paced environment
  • Comfortable with basic technology, including POS systems and mobile apps
  • Basic math and cash handling skills
  • Previous experience in retail, sales, or customer service preferred
  • Must be at least 16 years old
  • Available to work evenings, weekends, and holidays

Benefits

  • Competitive hourly wage
  • Free tokens and discounts
  • Fun and interactive work environment
  • Opportunities for growth and advancement
