How a party works

  1. Pick your arcade and package

    Choose your arcade above to see its packages and prices, or ask us for a custom party for any group size.

  2. Book or request your party

    Las Vegas and Easton Town Center packages book online at least 3 days ahead, and a 50% deposit holds your date. For International Market Place and custom parties, send a request with the form below and we’ll coordinate the details with you.

  3. Arrive and celebrate

    Your Party Host greets your group, gets everyone set up with tokens and keeps the party running, so you can enjoy the celebration.

Parties at every arcade

Each store is set up a little differently. Here’s what to expect at each one.

Fashion Show Mall, Las Vegas

A private party area with tables and chairs for your group. Bring your own food and cake, or add pizza and drinks when you book. On the second floor next to Dillard’s, with free parking in the mall garage.

AREA15, Las Vegas

A party area with tables and chairs, on the first floor of the main bunker past Meow Wolf’s Omega Mart. Food and cake can’t come from outside: they’re catered by AREA15’s in-house providers for an added cost, and we’ll set it up after you book.

Miracle Mile Shops, Las Vegas

Parties here are all about the games. There’s no room for tables and chairs, so food isn’t part of parties at this store. Want food? Book at Fashion Show Mall instead.

International Market Place, Honolulu

Games-only parties in Waikīkī. Packages are coming soon, so send us your date and group size and we’ll put together a custom party. Our Ala Moana Center arcade opens in December 2026.

Easton Town Center, Columbus

Parties with 70+ places to play, including Claw King, where one player runs ten claws at once. Packages run $350 to $750 for a 1-hour time slot, with 450 to 1,000 tokens to share. Bring your own food and cake, and we’ll set up a drop-zone table for your group.

Party FAQ

How much is a birthday party at Fantasy Claw Arcade?

In Las Vegas, party packages are $399 for up to 10 guests, $599 for up to 15 and $799 for up to 20. Each is 2 hours with a dedicated Party Host and 25 to 45 tokens per guest. At Easton Town Center in Columbus, packages are $350, $550 and $750, plus sales tax, for a 1-hour time slot with 450, 725 or 1,000 tokens to share, suggested for up to 10, 15 or 20 guests. Honolulu parties are custom-quoted while packages are finalized.

What’s included in a party package?

In Las Vegas, every package includes a dedicated Party Host, tokens for every guest, a birthday gift for the birthday guest, bottled water for every guest and a discount on extra tokens during the party. Bigger packages add the Wizard’s Quest scavenger hunt, the Claw Magic Tournament, the Plush Spell Challenge and more tokens. At Easton Town Center, packages include a 1-hour time slot and 450 to 1,000 tokens to share however you like. Ask about add-ons for guaranteed prizes.

How far ahead do I need to book?

Book Las Vegas and Easton Town Center packages online at least 3 days ahead. A 50% deposit holds your date, and you choose your date and time when you book. For Honolulu and custom parties, send a request through the form on this page and our team will coordinate with you.

Can we bring our own food and cake?

At Fashion Show Mall, yes: bring your own food and cake, or add pizza and drinks when you book. At AREA15, food and cake are catered by AREA15’s in-house providers for an added cost. At Easton Town Center, you’re welcome to bring your own food and cake. There’s no party area, but we set up a drop-zone table at the front of the store for your group. Miracle Mile Shops and International Market Place parties are games only.

Which arcade has a private party area?

Fashion Show Mall has a private party area with tables and chairs. AREA15 has a party area too. Our other stores don’t have a party area, so parties there center on the games.

How do tokens work at a party?

Each guest gets tokens with your package. Claw machines take 1 to 4 tokens per try, depending on the prize, and most take just 1. Gashapon capsule machines, at every arcade except Miracle Mile Shops, take 6 to 10 tokens. Extra tokens are discounted during the party.

Are parties only for kids?

Not at all. Plenty of our players are adults, so parties work just as well for teens and grown-ups. Full-arcade rentals are available for corporate events and team outings too.

Can we rent the whole arcade?

Yes. Every location can be rented for a private party or event. Use the form on this page to tell us your date and group size, and we’ll put together a quote.
